World happiness CSV analysis (2015-2019)
=========================================

Current validation is in validation_results.json. The 10 November 2026
country-held-out rerun reported R2 0.778830 and MAE 0.409176 score units
on 34 held-out countries. The five input CSV hashes are in that JSON.

These are associations within the committed files, not causal effects or
future-year predictions. Exact source lineage and data rights for the CSVs
remain unverified. The prior 0.747 R2 and feature-ranking summary were
from an earlier method and are not current validation results.
